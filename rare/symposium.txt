## Requirement coverage

- **Selectors:** element (`nav`, `th`), class (`.card`), ID (`#hero`, `#top`), descendant (`nav a`, `#hero h1`), `:hover`, and `::before` on `h2`.
- **Box model:** margin, padding, border, border-radius and `box-sizing: border-box` (the `*` rule).
- **Typography:** font-family, size, weight, text-align, text-transform and letter-spacing are all used.
- **Layout:** Flexbox is used for the nav, highlights, non-technical cards and contact. Grid is used for the technical events.
- **Positioning:** a sticky nav and a fixed back-to-top button.
- **Display:** `block` (labels), `inline` (`.price`), `inline-block` (`.logo`, buttons).
- **Effects:** transition, transform, box-shadow, text-shadow and opacity.
- **Responsive:** media queries for mobile, tablet and desktop.

The contact page has a commented-out spot for the static location image. If your faculty requires it, uncomment that line and the `img` styles will apply to it. Also replace the placeholder names, dates and prizes with your real details.
